Partyzra Music Persistence — route handoff fix

Behavior after this update:
- The site opens with no compact/global player visible.
- Music can only be initiated from the full controls on the Music page.
- Those Music-page controls drive the audio element in the persistent outer shell.
- While the visitor remains on Music, the compact player stays hidden.
- Navigating to Home or Photography does not replace/restart the audio. The same shell audio element keeps playing and the compact player appears there.
- Returning to Music hides the compact player again while playback continues and the full Music-page controls stay synchronized.
- A retry guard prevents an iframe timing race from accidentally falling back to local page audio.
