PARTYZRA — MUSIC PLAYBACK FIX v2.7.1

Fixes the Music-page Play buttons after the seamless route-handoff update.

Changes:
- Music page resolves the parent persistent-player API at click time instead of
  depending on a fragile one-time readiness handshake.
- Removed the parent player's explicit audio.load() before play(), which can
  cause mobile browsers to reject playback after a user tap.
- The parent shell remains the sole playback source when inside the site shell.
- Music continues seamlessly when navigating from Music to Home/Photography.
- Compact player remains hidden while on Music and appears off the Music page
  only after playback has been started.
- Cache-busted site-shell.js and music-persistence.js to prevent browsers from
  reusing the broken previous scripts.
- photos.js and cloudflare-worker/worker.js are unchanged.
