About

I make things that hold onto a feeling.

I’m a videographer, photographer, musician, and developer interested in the details that give a moment its character. My work moves between still images, sound, and motion, with the same goal: make something that feels lived-in and memorable.

This site is an evolving archive of personal work, collaborations, experiments, and selected professional projects.
