PARTYZRA HERO V7 — INVISIBLE SINGLE-POINT HANG

Replace only:
  css/style.css

WHAT CHANGED
- The two visible cords from V6 are completely removed.
- Each large word now behaves as though it hangs from ONE invisible point
  directly above the center of that word.
- The complete row no longer swings as one rigid object.
- The main word can subtly:
    • dip left-side down
    • dip right-side down
    • turn a fraction toward the left/right in 3D
    • rise/fall by only a couple pixels
- Timing is deliberately slow and slightly asymmetrical for a nostalgic,
  analog hanging-mobile / gallery-installation feeling.
- Observe, Listen, and Reminisce have different movement weights and periods.
- Photography / Music / Film stay calmer so the effect does not become busy.
- Steel, gold shimmer, ember/fire, spacing, fade-ins, and curated hero photos
  remain intact.

There is no visible string. The suspension point exists only in the motion.
